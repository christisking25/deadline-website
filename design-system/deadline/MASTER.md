# Deadline: Master Design System

Generated by ui-ux-pro-max, then merged with Deadline brand rules.
**Brand rules win on every conflict.** Conflicts are marked `[OVERRIDE]`.

Site: hitdeadline.com
Product: iOS screen-time enforcement app. Two features: Focus (commitment-based
locks released by proof) and TapLatch (a physical NFC card that locks and
unlocks apps with a tap).

---

## 1. Pattern (from skill, kept)

**Scroll-Triggered Storytelling.**

The page is a narrative, not a feature list. Order: hook, problem, journey, solution, climax CTA.

Rules carried over from the generator:
- The story must read fine with zero scroll effects. Animation is decoration, never content.
- Mini CTA at the end of chapters, one climax CTA at the end.
- Mobile: simplify animation, do not pin or scrub.
- DOM reading order stays complete and linear for screen readers.
- Pause scroll animation when offscreen.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, every chapter renders in its final readable state immediately.

Chapter map for this site:
1. Hook: Hero
2. Problem: The Call Out
3. Journey: How It Works
4. Solution: Focus, TapLatch, Location, Technology, Privacy
5. Climax: Final CTA

---

## 2. Style

Generator said **Liquid Glass**. Kept, but darkened and hardened.

`[OVERRIDE]` Final style: **Dark Cinematic Glass.**

- Translucent surfaces over near-black, backdrop blur 12 to 24px.
- Hairline borders at `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`, lifting to gold on hover.
- Large soft gold radial glows behind section anchors, low opacity, heavily blurred.
- Grain overlay across the whole page at 3 to 4% opacity.
- Lensing/refraction from the generator is reduced to: specular top edge highlight on cards and phone bezels only. No heavy distortion effects. They cost frames.
- Keywords kept: translucency, optical glass, system navigation. Dropped: fluid morphing (too playful for this tone).

Reference feel: Linear.app structure, Arc site warmth, Apple product page restraint, Cluely bluntness.

## 3. Color `[OVERRIDE: the iOS app wins entirely]`

The generator proposed a light theme (`#FAFAF9` background, `#A16207` accent). Rejected. Deadline is dark only, by intent.

**These values are lifted verbatim from `Deadline/UI/Theme.swift` (`enum Palette`) in the iOS project.** The site is not allowed to invent a colour. If the app changes, change it here and in `src/index.css` together.

| Token | Value | Swift name | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-bg` | `#0B0B0C` | `Palette.nearBlack` | Page background |
| `--color-surface` | `#141416` | `Palette.surface` | Cards, phone chassis |
| `--color-surface-2` | `#1C1C1F` | `Palette.surfaceRaised` | Raised rows, circular nav buttons |
| `--color-hairline` | `#2A2A2E` | `Palette.hairline` | Card borders, at 60% alpha |
| `--color-muted` | `#8A8A8E` | `Palette.muted` | Body copy, section labels |
| `--color-ink` | `#F4F4F5` | `Palette.ink` | Headlines, row titles |
| `--color-amber` | `#FBAE3C` | `Palette.amber` | The brand tint. CTAs, Focus accents, active tab |
| `--color-red` | `#FF453A` | `Palette.red` | Lockdown shields only |
| `--color-green` | `#30D158` | `Palette.green` | Proof accepted, Location Lock in force |
| `--color-blue` | `#5AA0FF` | `Palette.blue` | Gym commitments |

### Contrast, verified against `#0B0B0C`

| Pair | Ratio | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Amber on bg | 10.5:1 | AAA |
| bg on amber (buttons) | 10.5:1 | AAA |
| Ink on bg | 19.6:1 | AAA |
| Muted `#8A8A8E` on bg | 5.73:1 | AA |
| Muted on `#141416` | 5.33:1 | AA |

`#8A8A8E` is the floor. Never put a gray lighter than it.

### Discipline rules, inherited from the app
- **Red is deliberately narrow.** On the site it appears at: the "1" in the Call Out, and lockdown shields. Nowhere else.
- **Blue means a gym commitment**, never decoration.
- **Green means a lock in force or proof accepted**, never decoration.
- Amber is an accent, not a fill. Target under 10% of any viewport.
- No gradient text on headlines.

## 4. Typography `[OVERRIDE: match the app, not the generator]`

Generator returned Cormorant / Montserrat. Cormorant is a serif. Rejected.

The app renders essentially everything in **SF Pro Rounded** (`design: .rounded` in SwiftUI). The site has to match or the two stop reading as one product, so the web stack resolves the real thing on Apple devices and falls back to Nunito elsewhere.

| Role | Stack | Weights | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | `ui-rounded, "SF Pro Rounded", "Nunito"` | 700, 800 | `ui-rounded` is a CSS generic that resolves to SF Pro Rounded on Apple platforms. Nunito is the closest widely available rounded geometric. |
| Body | same | 400, 600 | The app uses rounded for most chrome. |
| Row text | `-apple-system, "Inter"` | 400, 500, 600 | Inside phone mockups only. The app uses plain SF (not rounded) for row titles and body, so the mockups do too. |

```
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Nunito:wght@400;600;700;800;900&family=Inter:wght@400;500;600&display=swap
```

## 5. Voice

Coach who does not accept excuses. Short sentences. Second person. Blunt.

- Say "You said you would." Not "Users often struggle with."
- No exclamation marks. The confidence comes from brevity.
- `[OVERRIDE]` No em dashes anywhere. Periods, commas, colons only.
- No lorem ipsum. Every string ships as final copy.
- Statistics are labelled as general estimates. We do not invent precision.

## 6. Motion

From the generator's GSAP catalog, implemented in Framer Motion.

| Use | Spec |
|---|---|
| Scroll reveal | opacity 0 to 1, y 24 to 0, 500ms, `power2.out` equivalent `[0.22,1,0.36,1]`, trigger at 85% viewport |
| Stagger | 80ms between children, max 8 children |
| Parallax | yPercent 5 to 15 only, decorative layers only, never text |
| Hover | 200ms, transform and border-color only |
| Counters | 1.8s ease-out, `tabular-nums`, starts on viewport entry, runs once |

Hard rules:
- Animate `transform` and `opacity` only. Never `height`, `top`, `width`.
- `will-change: transform` on parallax layers only, removed when settled.
- No pinned sections. Everything scrolls natively.
- Mobile under 768px: no pinning, no scrub, no mouse-tilt. Reveals only.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` kills marquees, parallax, scrub, pulse, and counters. Counters print their final value. Everything renders visible.

## 7. Components

**Glass card.** `--surface` at 60% alpha, blur 16px, 1px `--border`, radius 16px. Hover: border to `--border-gold`, translateY -4px, gold glow at 0.12 alpha. 200ms.

**Primary button.** Solid gold, `#0A0A0A` text, radius 12px, 52px tall. Hover: brightness 1.08 plus 0 0 32px gold glow. Focus: 2px white ring at 3px offset.

**Ghost button.** Transparent, 1px `--border`, white text. Hover: border gold, background `rgba(212,168,83,0.06)`.

**Phone mockup.** Pure CSS and SVG. 380x780 at 1x. 48px radius outer, 8px `#1C1C1C` bezel, specular top-edge highlight, Dynamic Island pill, status bar with time, signal, battery. No stock images.

**Icons.** Lucide, 1.5px stroke, 20 or 24px, `currentColor`. `[OVERRIDE]` SVG only. Zero emoji as icons, anywhere, including inside mockups.

## 9. Pre-delivery checklist

From the generator, plus brand additions.

- [ ] No emojis as icons. SVG only.
- [ ] `cursor-pointer` on every clickable element.
- [ ] Hover states transition in 150 to 300ms.
- [ ] Text contrast 4.5:1 minimum. Dark theme verified in section 3.
- [ ] Focus states visible for keyboard nav.
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
- [ ] Responsive at 375, 768, 1024, 1440.
- [ ] `[BRAND]` No em dashes in any copy.
- [ ] `[BRAND]` No lorem ipsum. All copy final.
- [ ] `[BRAND]` Red used only for lockdown moments.
- [ ] `[BRAND]` Below-fold images lazy loaded.
- [ ] `[BRAND]` Meta title, description, OG image, hourglass favicon present.
- [ ] `[BRAND]` No invented testimonials. The section stays an empty state until real App Store reviews exist.

### Avoid (generator)
Cheap visuals. Fast animations. If an animation feels snappy, it is too fast for this brand. Minimum 400ms on reveals.
